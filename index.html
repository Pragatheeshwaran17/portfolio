Project 01● Student app live

EduPrepNEET / JEE practice test platform for a coaching academy

A coaching academy owner had a question bank of over one lakh questions sitting in PDFs. EduPrep turns it into timed, subject-wise MCQ tests that students take on their phones, with instant scores and a breakdown by Physics, Chemistry and Biology.

  • RolesThree levels: a super admin uploads the question PDFs, academy admins run and monitor tests for their students, and students log in and take them.
  • BusinessBuilt to be resold. The owner licenses the bank to other academies, with access scaled to the number of question sets they buy.
  • CostRuns on free tiers end to end. Vercel hosts the app and a self-hosted PocketBase holds the data, so nobody touches the database directly.

Project 02● Testing build in progress

FitDeskGym management for independent gyms and fitness studios in India

Most small gyms still run on a register and a WhatsApp group. FitDesk gives the owner one screen for members, plans, payments and renewals, so nobody slips past their expiry date and the month's revenue is never a guess.

  • DashboardActive members, renewals due this week, today's check-ins and monthly revenue at a glance.
  • AccessRole-based: owners see the money, front-desk staff see members and check-ins. Each gym's account is provisioned and isolated.
  • DetailsA duplicate phone number warns the front desk but lets them override it. Renewal reminders follow a set policy, so follow-ups don't depend on memory.
